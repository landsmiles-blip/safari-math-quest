## 🌟 Game Structure
Both games feature 2 distinct play environments:
1. **Mode 1: Training Grounds (Classroom)**
   - 5 pedagogical training camps.
   - Gentle female audio teacher narration, lesson hints, and scratchpad.
   - Unlimited retries to build confidence.
   - Completing all 5 camps earns a passport stamp and unlocks Mode 2.
2. **Mode 2: Safari Exam Challenge**
   - 15 randomized challenges per playthrough.
   - Focused locked screen until all 15 questions are completed.
   - Replay difficulty tiers that scale in challenge every play.
   - High-resolution, printable A4 Royal Certificate with accurate gold stars.
